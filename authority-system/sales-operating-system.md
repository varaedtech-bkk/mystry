# Sales Operating System - PRISM Authority Sprint

This document makes your sales process repeatable from first touch to signed agreement.

---

## 1) Pipeline Stages

1. **Prospect Identified**
2. **Contacted**
3. **Replied**
4. **Discovery Booked**
5. **Qualified**
6. **Proposal Sent**
7. **Negotiation**
8. **Won / Lost**

Rule: no stage advances without explicit evidence.

---

## 2) Qualification Scorecard (0-2 each, max 10)

### A) Strategic Need
- 0 = unclear need
- 1 = moderate pain
- 2 = urgent authority/discovery gap

### B) Offer Complexity
- 0 = commodity/simple offer
- 1 = moderate complexity
- 2 = high complexity needing engineered messaging

### C) Decision Access
- 0 = no decision-maker access
- 1 = partial access
- 2 = founder/exec involved

### D) Budget Readiness
- 0 = no budget
- 1 = tentative budget
- 2 = approved budget range

### E) Execution Readiness
- 0 = no internal support
- 1 = limited support
- 2 = clear point-of-contact and fast feedback loop

**Close threshold:** 7+ recommended for proposal.

---

## 3) Discovery Call Flow (30 Minutes)

### Minute 0-3: Frame
"I will ask a few questions to map whether a PRISM sprint is the right fit. If yes, I will outline next steps."

### Minute 3-12: Current State
- How do prospects currently discover you?
- Which technical strengths are hardest to communicate?
- Where does the sales cycle stall most often?

### Minute 12-20: Gap Mapping
- What buyer questions are not being captured in AI/search?
- What proof assets exist today?
- How consistent is your current distribution engine?

### Minute 20-25: Impact
- What happens if this is not fixed in the next 90 days?
- What would make this engagement clearly successful?

### Minute 25-30: Next Step
- Confirm fit score
- Define scope and timeline
- Schedule proposal review

---

## 4) Discovery Questions Bank

Use 5-8 per call:

- Which segment is highest value but hardest to convert?
- What phrases do prospects repeat before buying?
- Which channels bring low-quality vs high-quality leads?
- What does your team publish that actually influences deals?
- Where do prospects still misunderstand your differentiation?
- How do you currently measure authority, if at all?

---

## 5) Offer Framing Script

"You do not need more disconnected tactics.  
You need an authority architecture that aligns positioning, retrieval, proof, and distribution.  
The PRISM Sprint builds this in 21 days with measurable output and a clear operating routine."

---

## 6) Objection Handling

### "We already do SEO."
Response:
"Great. PRISM does not replace SEO. It aligns technical positioning and proof so SEO output becomes higher trust and more conversion-relevant."

### "This feels like marketing, not product."
Response:
"In technical markets, authority is a product translation problem. We make your real product value legible to buyers and answer engines."

### "Can we just do content?"
Response:
"Content without positioning and proof creates noise. PRISM ensures every asset has strategic function."

### "Price is high."
Response:
"Totally fair. The cost of invisible authority is longer cycles and lower close rates. PRISM is built to reduce that drag quickly and measurably."

---

## 7) Proposal Review Script

1. Reconfirm business objective and timeline
2. Reconfirm current authority bottlenecks
3. Walk through scope by PRISM phases
4. Clarify responsibilities and checkpoints
5. Ask for decision timeline and procurement requirements

Close question:
"Is there anything in scope, sequence, or investment that would prevent us from starting this sprint this month?"

---

## 8) Daily Sales Rhythm (Mon-Fri)

- 45 min prospect research and list building
- 60 min new outbound
- 30 min follow-ups
- 30 min call prep and post-call notes
- 15 min pipeline update

---

## 9) Weekly Review Questions

- Which message variants got the highest reply quality?
- Which ICP segment booked most calls?
- Where are deals stalling by stage?
- Which objection appears most often?
- What should change next week: targeting, script, or offer framing?
