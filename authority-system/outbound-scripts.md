## 12) Personalization Prompts (Do Before Sending)

- Which technical claim is strongest on their site?
- Which buyer question is not explicitly answered?
- Where does proof currently break (no demo, no case, no evidence)?
- Which channel seems active but under-structured?

Never send a message without at least one specific observation.
